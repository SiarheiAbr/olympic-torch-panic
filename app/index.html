How to Play

🎯 Goal

You carry the Olympic flame toward Los Angeles — the runner moves forward on their own. Keep the flame alive as long as you can. Reach 5000 m with the flame still burning to win.

🕹️ Controls

Desktop: move the mouse left/right, or hold A / D.

Mobile: drag left/right anywhere on the screen.

Pause: Esc or P (switching tabs pauses automatically).

🛡️ The shield

The torch rotates around the runner and blocks hazards only on the side it faces (the white arc). Every hazard is telegraphed first — a red warning shows where it will strike from. Point the torch at the warning before the hazard hits!

⚠️ Hazards

  • 💨 Wind gusts — blow in from the left or right
  • 🌧️ Rain showers — pour down from above
  • 🛸 Drones — downdraft from the upper diagonals
  • 🏐 Beach balls — a single hard hit from fans along the route
  • 🎆 Fireworks — three quick bursts from the front

Later stages throw several hazards at once — sometimes from opposite sides. You can't block everything: choose what hurts most.

🔥 The flame

Unblocked hazards drain the flame; at zero it goes out and the run ends. The flame slowly recovers during calm moments, so a weak flame can be nursed back. When it turns critical, the screen pulses red — act fast.

🏆 Scoring

Distance is your score. The top 10 runs and your longest flame survival are saved on this device.
